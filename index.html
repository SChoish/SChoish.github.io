Offline Goal-Conditioned Reinforcement Learning

PathBridger: Subgoal Bridges for Offline Goal-Conditioned Reinforcement Learning

Suhyun Choi, Seonbin Cho, and Songnam Hong

A hierarchical offline GCRL approach that constructs explicit state-space bridges, decodes them into short executable action chunks through inverse dynamics, and replans during execution. Evaluated on long-horizon OGBench navigation and manipulation tasks, including limited-data settings.

Code ↗ AAAI 2027 submission

Work in Progress 2026–Present

Reliable Policy Optimization

Calibrated Adaptive Multi-step Proximal Improvement (CAPO)

A study of calibrated, bounded policy refinement in offline RL when critic estimates are imperfect and distribution shift grows away from dataset support. The project develops criteria for reliable improvement that account for value-estimation uncertainty and optimization error.
